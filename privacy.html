MURMUR

Privacy — what leaves this browser, and what never does.

Back to the app

The short version

MURMUR has no server of its own. There is no account system, no analytics, no cookie, no logging endpoint. Your images and messages stay in this tab unless you explicitly ask a Pollinations model to look at one.

What stays on your device

  • Images. A cover you generate is decoded from base64 in this tab. A file you drop in is read with FileReader-equivalent browser APIs. Neither is uploaded anywhere by MURMUR.
  • The message. It is embedded in the pixels and, if you set one, encrypted with WebCrypto (PBKDF2 → AES-GCM) before embedding. It is never sent to a model and never leaves the tab.
  • The output PNG. Created with canvas.toBlob and handed to your downloads. It is not stored on a server.
  • Your Pollen key. Stored in this origin's localStorage under murmurKey. “Disconnect” deletes it. It is only ever sent as an Authorization: Bearer header to gen.pollinations.ai — never in a URL, never to a third party.

What is sent to Pollinations

Only when you press a button that needs it:

  • Generate cover — your text prompt, the model id and a size, to gen.pollinations.ai/v1/images/generations. This costs Pollen and is attributed to your own balance.
  • Ask a model (Seek tab, opt-in checkbox) — a 512 px JPEG copy of the inspected image plus a fixed instruction, to gen.pollinations.ai/v1/chat/completions. That copy contains the image, including any payload you just hid in it, at reduced resolution.
  • Connect / catalogue — the OAuth device flow at enter.pollinations.ai, then a GET /v1/models to see which models your balance may use.

Pollinations' own handling of those requests is described in their privacy notice. MURMUR cannot make promises about a service it does not run; it can only be precise about what it hands over.

What MURMUR does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, error reporting or “usage pings”.
  • No cookies. The only persistent storage is the two localStorage keys (murmurKey, murmurModel).
  • No third-party scripts, fonts or CDNs. Everything is served from this origin.
  • No hidden model calls. The Bench, the codec and the deterministic detectors call nothing at all.

Clearing it

Press Disconnect to remove the key, or clear site data in your browser. Downloaded PNGs are ordinary files under your control — deleting them from disk deletes the message with them.

A hidden payload is not a secret-sharing scheme on its own. Anyone who has the file can read an unencrypted payload with the same open codec; use a passphrase if that matters.
